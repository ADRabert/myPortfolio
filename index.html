About Me

I am an aspiring web developer & software engineer who likes to dabble in various open-source software projects from time to time. I was born & raised in the USA (Pennsylvania, to be specific), & have a passion for getting things done as efficiently as possible. I started my software tinkering journey by trying to get some games to run better on an old laptop from 2014 passed down to me. Since then, I've learned how to create & manage my own computer systems, & dial everything just right to squeeze as much potential out of underpowered hardware as possible. Thanks to this skill, I still have that old laptop to this day, & use it to run modern software & host servers for friends.

This site is designed to work without JavaScript. Ancient computers welcome!
